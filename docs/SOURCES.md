The Bangkok planning portal's summary PDF at `cityplangis.bangkok.go.th/cpdPortal/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/06_cpd56.pdf` returned HTTP502 during research. Numerical zoning values were therefore read from the reproduced Gazette text and the relevant pages visually checked. The ASA legal texts were read and checked at the cited clauses; this is not a search for every potentially applicable local ordinance.

Design assumptions: floor height3.2/3.8/3.4m for condominium/office/hotel; net efficiency75/80/65%; four podium floors; podium net efficiency40%; tower target40% of plot; minimum plate60m²; model cap70floors; supported plot100–80,000m²; up to100corners. These are modelling choices, not legal thresholds. Six-metre near-road tolerance is a geometric matching assumption, not proof of legal access.
